# Option C Research Specification: Supply Chain 3-Way PO Matching & Ind AS 2 Perpetual FIFO Inventory

> **Document Type:** Research & Feature Specification (Deferred Milestone)  
> **Feature IDs:** `FEAT-SC-01` (3-Way PO Matching) & `FEAT-INV-05` (Ind AS 2 FIFO Inventory)  
> **Status:** Documented for Future Implementation  
> **Target Audience:** Physical Goods Traders, Manufacturers, Supply Chain Leads, and CAs

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Option C extends `b00k5` from a service/digital bookkeeping OS into a full physical goods procurement and inventory platform. It consists of:
1. **Supply Chain 3-Way Matching Engine**: Purchase Orders (PO) $\leftrightarrow$ Goods Inward Notes (GRN / MRN) $\leftrightarrow$ Vendor Tax Bills, with discrepancy tolerance checks and landed cost capitalization.
2. **Ind AS 2 / AS 2 Perpetual FIFO Inventory Engine**: Batch/serial lot tracking, real-time Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) double-entry voucher posting, and statutory Section 17(5)(h) Input Tax Credit (ITC) reversal watchdogs.

---

## 2. Pillar 1: Supply Chain 3-Way PO Matching (`FEAT-SC-01`)

### 2.1 The 3-Way Matching Invariant
In physical goods procurement, paying vendor bills without verifying actual receipt of goods leads to overpayments, phantom billing, and supplier disputes.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        3-Way Matching Pipeline                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                        │
│   1. Purchase Order (PO): Issued to vendor specifying items,          │
│      quantities, agreed unit prices, and delivery terms.               │
│                           │                                            │
│                           ▼                                            │
│   2. Goods Inward Note (GRN): Generated by warehouse receiving dock.    │
│      Records actual received quantity, damaged items, and batch nos.   │
│                           │                                            │
│                           ▼                                            │
│   3. Vendor Tax Bill: Supplier's tax invoice demanding payment.        │
│                           │                                            │
│                           ▼                                            │
│   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐     │
│   │           3-Way Match Verification Engine                    │     │
│   │  Checks:                                                     │     │
│   │  - Quantity Invoiced == Quantity Received in GRN             │     │
│   │  - Price Invoiced == Agreed Unit Price in PO                 │     │
│   │  - Tolerances: Configurable threshold (e.g. max ±2%)         │     │
│   │  Status: 'MATCHED' -> Bill Approved for Payment              │     │
│   │          'DISCREPANCY' -> Payment Blocked; Issue Debit Note   │     │
│   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘     │
│                                                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2 Landed Cost Allocation Engine
Under Ind AS 2 (Paragraph 10), costs of purchase comprise purchase price, import duties, handling costs, and freight directly attributable to the acquisition of goods.
* **Mechanism:** Apportions separate freight/shipping bills across inventory batches based on either **weight** or **value proportion**.
* **Accounting Entry:**
  * Debit: `Inventory Valuation Account (Code 1050)` (Purchase Price + Allocated Freight)
  * Credit: `Trade Payables - Vendor (Code 2040)` (Goods Price)
  * Credit: `Trade Payables - Logistics / Customs (Code 2041)` (Allocated Freight)

## 3. Pillar 2: Ind AS 2 Perpetual FIFO Inventory & Real-Time COGS Engine (`FEAT-INV-05`)

### 3.1 Statutory Standards: Ind AS 2 / AS 2 / ICDS II
* **Prohibited Methods:** LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) is strictly prohibited under Indian GAAP, Ind AS 2, and Income Tax ICDS II.
* **Permitted Methods:** FIFO (First-In, First-Out) or Weighted Average Cost (WAC).
* **Valuation Rule:** Lower of Cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV).

### 3.2 Real-Time COGS Double-Entry Accounting
Whenever a sales tax invoice is issued for physical stock items, the system automatically draws from the oldest available FIFO inventory layer and posts:
* **Debit:** `Cost of Goods Sold (Account 5010)` (Quantity $\times$ Historical Unit Landed Cost)
* **Credit:** `Stock-in-Hand / Finished Goods (Account 1050)` (Inventory reduction)

This ensures that the gross profit on the monthly Profit & Loss statement reflects true matching of revenue with the cost of items sold.

### 3.3 Section 17(5)(h) Blocked Credit Watchdog
Under Section 17(5)(h) of the CGST Act, 2017, Input Tax Credit (ITC) must be reversed if goods are:
1. Lost or stolen.
2. Destroyed or written off.
3. Disposed of by way of gift or free samples.
* **System Action:** When stock adjustments (damage/expiry/theft) are recorded, the system automatically posts an ITC reversal voucher:
  * Debit: `Abnormal Stock Loss (Expense 4090)`
  * Credit: `Stock-in-Hand (Asset 1050)`
  * Credit: `Input GST Reversal Account (Liability 2104)`

## 4. Why Deferred in Favor of Option A
* **User Profile Fit:** Companies currently adopting `b00k5` for local self-hosting are primarily software companies, consulting practices, and digital service agencies that have zero physical warehouse inventory.
* **Maintainability:** Option C requires 12 new relational tables, complex concurrency locks for lot depletion, and warehouse UI components. Deferring this keeps the codebase ultra-lean and focused on sovereign banking, payroll, and GST.
